## Weekly head-to-head matchups (idea, 2026-10-01)

Every Monday teams are paired off, and whoever gains more points that week wins the matchup.

**Why:** season standings move slowly, so most weeks nobody is playing for anything, least of all
teams near the bottom. A weekly matchup gives every owner a fresh contest and a specific rival to
beat (and talk trash to) each week.

**Built mostly on what exists:**

- Scoring: the 30-minute ESPN sync already records each team's point gains; a matchup compares two
  teams' gains from Monday to Sunday.
- Feed: a Monday "This week's matchups" league post, plus lead-change posts as scores move.
- Push: a fourth alert type ("Your opponent just pulled ahead by 4", "You won your matchup").
- Weekly digest: open with last week's results and this week's pairings.
- Trades and free agents gain a short-term reason: moves to win this week, not just the season.

**Cheap extras:** a weekly win-loss record on each profile, and a "Rivalry" tag when the same two
teams have met three or more times.

**Open question for Matt:** how teams are paired: random, standings neighbors (closer games), or a
fixed schedule people can look ahead at. Also what happens with an odd number of teams (a bye, or
one team plays the league average).
